REGIONAL GROWTH ANALYSIS – TASK 30

Objective
Compare regional growth rates over time using consistent periods and identify meaningful regional trends.

Dataset
Superstore-style dataset covering 2021–2024 with 1,600 transactions.

Method
Annual sales were aggregated by Region and Year. Year-over-year growth was calculated as:
(Current Year Sales - Previous Year Sales) / Previous Year Sales × 100

Five Insights
1. West had the highest 2024 sales.
2. East had the strongest 2023–2024 growth.
3. Central had the weakest 2023–2024 growth.
4. South had the strongest 2021–2024 CAGR.
5. Growth percentages should always be reviewed with the underlying sales base to avoid small-base interpretation errors.

Business Recommendation
Prioritize regions with strong sustained growth while investigating regions with slowing growth. Compare both absolute sales and percentage growth before making resource-allocation decisions.

Interview Preparation
Growth rate can mislead when the comparison base is very small. A small increase in absolute sales can produce a large percentage increase.
